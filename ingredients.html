Where it comes from

Sourcing

Our beef comes from free-range, grass-fed cattle raised in the United States. Our salmon is wild-caught. Our chicken is sourced domestically and arrives fresh, not reconstituted from powder.

We have used most of our suppliers for over a decade. That matters more than any certification we could put on a bag: we know their names, we visit, and we can trace a batch back to a delivery.

Everything is made in our own kitchen. We do not white-label anybody else's product and sell it as ours.

How it's made

Slow, mostly

There is no clever technology here. The difference is time and rejecting more than we keep.

  • 1
    Inspected on arrival Every delivery is checked by hand before it goes anywhere near the kitchen.
  • 2
    Slow-smoked or slow-dried Low and slow, over hours. Fast drying makes a chew that shatters and gets swallowed.
  • 3
    Graded, not just packed Anything cracked, thin or off-colour comes out. It is a lot of product. That's the job.
  • 4
    Batch coded Every bag carries a code that tells us the day it was made and where the raw material came from.
Feeding & safety

Sensible rules for hard chewers

Chews are safe when they're supervised. These are the same rules we give the shops we supply, and they haven't changed in twenty years.

  • Always supervise your dog with any chew, every time.
  • Take the chew away once it is small enough to swallow whole.
  • Match the chew to the dog — a 6-inch stick is not for a 90 lb power chewer.
  • Keep fresh water available while they work at it.
  • Introduce anything new slowly, especially the richer products.
  • Treats should stay under 10% of daily calories.

If your dog has a health condition, is pregnant, or is on medication, talk to your vet before adding a functional supplement. Our functional chews support normal health — they are not a treatment for disease and we won't pretend otherwise.
