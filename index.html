Our story

Twenty years behind other people's labels

My father started this company twenty years ago with one smoker and a list of six shops. For two decades we made treats that went out under other brands' names.

I grew up in that kitchen. I know which batches he'd throw out and why, and I know that nothing about the recipes needed changing. What needed changing was that you couldn't buy them without going through somebody else first.

So we put our own name on the bag. That's the whole story.
